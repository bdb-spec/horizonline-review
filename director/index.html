Raptus2025

Sci-Fi Thriller / Dark Comedy · Writer / Director

Ksenia Solo. Apple TV worldwide. The New York Times.

Ksenia Solo, Nolan Gerard Funk, Christina Cox

After a brutal assault, Sarah enters an experimental android-therapy program. Her new companion begins to blur care with control.

A film about assault, android obsession, and the violence people hide behind care. The material is dangerous and the tone never flinches. That only works when your cast commits completely. Ksenia Solo delivered a performance that demanded real trust between actor and director. Nolan Gerard Funk embodied the design perfectly.

Name cast, international distribution, and thematic ambition.

“Lurid and darkly funny. A delirious, uncomfortable back and forth that recalls the unearthly soapiness of Malignant.”

The Christmas Spirit2022

Comedy / Thriller / Horror · Writer / Director / Editor

An original take on the Christmas movie. Apple TV.

Zion Forrest Lee, Nicolas Grimes, Christian Potenza

A lone man with the Christmas Spirit trapped in his head must kidnap a teenage girl in order to save Christmas.

This is what happens when you take a Christmas movie and refuse to follow any of the usual formulas. Original story, original tone, original rules. The audience either gets it or they don’t. They got it.

Genre invention. Proof that the voice isn’t limited to one lane.

Cherrypicker2021

a.k.a. Ankle Biters

Horror Comedy · Writer / Director

Ensemble cast. Colin Mochrie. Distributed.

Colin Mochrie, Zion Forrest Lee, Marianthi Evans

A group of friends discovers that the babysitters they hired have a much darker agenda.

The leap from solo filmmaker to ensemble director. Colin Mochrie required a director who could hold comedic timing and horror tension in the same scene. Comedy earns trust. Horror spends it. Audiences loved it.

Ensemble direction, tone control across genres, commercial distribution.

“A pitch black comedy with serious consequences. I love the way this film really has a no fucks given attitude.”

Salvation2019

Thriller · Writer / Director / DP / Editor / Production Designer

Wrote. Directed. Shot. Edited. Designed.

Julian Richings, Zion Forrest Lee

An apocalypse survivor in search of his lost daughter is drawn into a deadly creation myth.

Punk rock filmmaking. Made with a crew of director, soundman, and actors. Shot in Detroit, Canada, and Iraq where footage of a naked man climbing a ziggurat was smuggled out of a warzone under threat of a ten year prison sentence. A demonstration of fearless storytelling and flat out commitment. Winner: Best Film, Best Cinematography, Best Production Design, Hollywood North Film Awards.

Total creative self-sufficiency. Can build a film from nothing.

“Excellent film!”
Bruce La Bruce

In His Own Words

A good director can create his own genre. That’s what I’ve done.

Salvation was made with no crew and no budget in Detroit, Canada, and Iraq. Cherrypicker put Colin Mochrie in a horror comedy where the kids are the monsters. The Christmas Spirit turned Christmas into something nobody had seen before. Raptus put Ksenia Solo opposite an android on Apple TV worldwide and got named by The New York Times.

Four films. A genre all their own.

FERTILITY is next. In pre-production.
