Background

Horizon Line Film Company is a Canadian studio producing a multi-genre slate of feature films for the global market — commercially-driven pictures built around established talent, each fully financed before cameras roll.

The company develops, finances, and produces across action, thriller, horror, comedy, drama, and animation, working with distribution and finance partners including Warner Bros., Red Sea Media, and Gala Film.
